Privacy

Privacy at a control layer

Find Anything's job is to respect boundaries. The same discipline applies to how it treats your data. This page summarizes the principles; it is a product summary, not a legal contract.

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Product examples are synthetic Acme AI data. The access-request form sends only what you type into it to the application's waitlist API (via a server-side relay on this domain) so we can review your request — nothing is transmitted until you press submit, and work emails are encrypted at rest by the application backend.

Least privilege by design

Retrieval runs inside the permissions the agent already holds. Find Anything never reads a source that is out of scope, and it cannot widen access on its own.

Document contents stay in your sources

  • Traces and receipts record what ran and why — scope, ranking, conflicts, coverage — not the private contents of documents.
  • An excluded source contributes no content to a result — no text, no snippets, no quotes. Its name can appear in coverage accounting only when it was inventoried during previously authorized access.
  • We do not copy your documents into marketing, demos, or fixtures. Every example here is fictional Acme AI data.

Data we would process (product)

To operate the connector, the product processes the minimum needed to retrieve and rank in-scope content and to produce receipts you can audit. Access is granted through the source's own consent flow and can be revoked there at any time.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal data.
  • We don't train on your private documents without explicit agreement.
  • We don't claim access to sources you haven't connected, or integrations we haven't shipped.

Your controls

  • Revoke connector access from the source (e.g. Google) at any time.
  • Request export or deletion of account data.
  • Review receipts to see exactly what any retrieval did.
