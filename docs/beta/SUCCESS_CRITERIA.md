# Private-beta success criteria

- Invitation-to-activation: at least 70%.
- Successful Drive connection: at least 90% of activated users.
- Successful first indexing: at least 85%, with under 5% failed files.
- Median time to first useful result: under 5 minutes.
- Search success: at least 70% of intentional searches open a useful result or receive positive feedback.
- Zero-result rate: below 25%, excluding deliberate no-result tests.
- Evidence trust: at least 4/5 average.
- Weekly return usage: at least 40% during a four-week beta.
- API interest: at least 30% request an integration conversation.
- Willingness to pay: at least 3 of the first 5 testers name a credible budget or paid pilot.
