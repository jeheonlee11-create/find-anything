# Find Anything beta guide

Find Anything searches the Google Drive files your connected account can access and shows exact evidence with a link to the original file. Drive access is read-only.

## Get started

1. Sign in with your invited ChatGPT account.
2. Create a workspace and choose **Connect Google Drive**.
3. Review Google's read-only permission screen.
4. Choose **Start indexing**. You may leave the page; saved progress is preserved.
5. Search for a title, filename, phrase, or words you remember.
6. Open **Exact evidence preview** or **Open source** to verify a result.

## Current limitations

- Search is deterministic literal full-text search, not semantic search.
- Some file formats are unsupported.
- Very large Drives may finish in several safe background batches.
- Results only include files currently authorized for the connected account.

## Report a problem

After several searches, use **Did you find what you needed?** or **Share feedback**. Describe what you expected without pasting private document text.
