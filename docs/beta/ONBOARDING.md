# Five-minute onboarding

1. Open the site and choose **Connect Google Drive**.
2. Sign in with the invited ChatGPT account when prompted. Sign-in is a step
   inside this flow, not a separate destination.
3. Approve the listed read-only Google permissions. A workspace is created for
   you automatically if you do not have one; the consent screen appears only if
   your account is on the current invitation list.
4. Click **Start indexing** and wait for the run to complete. You may leave the
   page — progress is checkpointed.
5. Search for an exact phrase you remember.
6. Open **Exact evidence**, **Why this result?**, the trace, and the source.
7. Use **Share feedback** if anything is missing or confusing.
8. Disconnect Drive from **Settings** when finished if you do not want the
   connection retained. Settings lists exactly what disconnecting deletes.

If your account is not yet on the invitation list, step 3 does not happen: you
land on the invite-only page and can request access there instead. No Google
authorization is started, and the page tells you nothing about Google-side
configuration.
