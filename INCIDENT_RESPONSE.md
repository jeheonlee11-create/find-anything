# Incident response

Severity 0: confirmed credential/customer-data exposure or cross-tenant access. Immediately disable the affected path, preserve logs, rotate credentials, notify the incident lead and counsel, and begin the applicable notification process.

Severity 1: major outage, permission enforcement uncertainty, failed deletion, or corrupted index. Fail closed, stop connector jobs, communicate status, and assign an incident commander.

Record timeline, scope, detection, containment, recovery, customer impact, evidence, and follow-ups. Never paste customer content or secrets into tickets. Roles, paging provider, security contact, and notification obligations require manual setup.
